Planning breaks down in the hand-offs: between the forecast and the inventory policy, between the plan and the system that produces it, between the planning team and the people who run the data.

We work across the whole chain — designing the planning process and building the tools and data behind it at the same time, so they fit. Where it helps, we bring [Demand Pilot](/products/demand-pilot/); where you already have tools, we make them work harder.
