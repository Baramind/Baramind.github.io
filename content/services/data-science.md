A model that works in a notebook is the start, not the finish. The real work is making it reliable on live data, explaining it to the people who depend on it, and keeping it accurate as conditions change.

We build data science for that second half: evaluated against a sensible baseline, deployed with monitoring, and designed so your team can understand and own it.
