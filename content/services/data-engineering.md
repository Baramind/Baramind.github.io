Most data problems look like model problems from the outside. Forecasts drift, dashboards disagree, and AI pilots stall — usually because the data underneath is late, inconsistent or nobody is sure where a number came from.

We build the data layer so that stops happening: tested pipelines, a model of the business people agree on, and the monitoring to know when something changes. It's the same foundation our own products run on.
