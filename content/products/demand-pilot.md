Most planning teams already have a forecast. What they lack is one they trust. When numbers move without explanation, planners override them — and the model quietly becomes a suggestion nobody follows.

Demand Pilot starts from the other end: a forecast is only useful if the people who own the plan can see why it says what it says, and how confident it is. Today it covers demand planning. Inventory, supply and S&OP follow, so that one system carries the plan from demand signal to supply decision.

Demand Pilot is in beta. We're working closely with a small number of teams to shape it.
